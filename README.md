# Ecosystem Map
An interactive visualization of the funding landscape and intellectual communities surrounding accountability and repair from extant and emerging digital and AI harms.

[Ecosystem Map](https://center-on-resilience-digital-justice.github.io/ecosystem-map/)



# Our Research Questions
- How might we understand where the funding opportunities are to sustain intellectual communities under surveillance? 
- How might we approach mapping as a method to connect, search and protect the work of intellectual communities under surveillance? 
- How might we connect the surveilled intellectual communities we are researching within to safe funders? 
- How might we identify risk factors of a funder to a surveilled intellectual community? 
- How might we identify the funding needs overlap of the intellectual communities we are researching within?
